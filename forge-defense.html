Bestiary — Know What's Coming

Every creature here is canon to Bloodline Forge: The Awakened Saga™. Now drawn in full — learn their silhouettes, their value, and their threat.

🌙

Moonwraith — 100 pts

Pale winged spectre with glowing violet eyes. Fast, drifting, low health. Comes in numbers early. Lore: A shadow that lags behind the dead — it drifts where the light thins, and it does not forget a face it has followed.

🐺

Glasshound — 150 pts

Shard-backed wolf, emerald eyes, zigzag charge. Two hits to shatter. Breach damage: 12 Forge HP. Lore: Born where lantern glass shattered under pressure — it runs on the hum, and the glass sings before it strikes.

💀

Boneharrow — “The Unburied Dead” — 200 pts

Horned bone golem, ember heart. Slow, armored, 3 hits. Breach damage: 18 Forge HP. Lore: The Unburied Dead, gathered bone by bone where no rite was spoken. Its ember heart burns for a burial it will never receive.

🌿

Living Sigil — 250 pts

A walking rune of the Forge's own power, turned wild. Pulses side to side. 2 hits, high value. Lore: One Sigil, walking. A fragment of the Forge's own mark, unbound — every bloodline that meets it is measured.

🦷

Thornjaw — 300 pts

Thorn-mawed bruiser, 4 hits, charges faster as Trials rise. Prioritize it. Breach: 22 HP. Lore: A double-hinged maw grown through briar and bone. It does not bite to feed — it bites to open the way for the Crown.

👑

Seris Thorne — ELITE • 500 pts

Crowned queen of thorns. 6 hits, fast, from Trial III. Every 5th Trial she can lead as a Trial Boss — bigger, tougher, guaranteed power-up. Lore: She smiles like she's got nothing to hide, and that is the most dangerous thing about her.

✨

Lyra Vale — Blessing • +400 & +25 Forge

Not an enemy. Golden and glowing — shoot her blessing orb to collect +400 points and +25 Forge HP. Lore: Lyra Vale carries the first spark into a world of ember and shadow — mender of lanterns, keeper of the Vale line.

🐉

Nightscale Drake

Lore: Scales black as a forge at midnight, breath cold enough to still a flame. Where a Nightscale passes, embers dim and even the Crown's riders give it the road.

🐍

Bloodvine Serpent

Lore: “In the wild only a snake. In Crown hands it is policy.” The Crown does not hunt. It cultivates — and the Bloodvine is what it grows in the dark between roots.

🦋

Lumen Moth

Lore: Drawn to lit glass and humming lanterns. Small, silent, and impossible to lie to — a Lumen Moth settles only where the light is true.

🦌

Hollow Stag

Lore: Antlers like bare branches in winter, hollow where a heart should echo. It gives itself so the herd — and the bloodline — can stand taller.

🪽

Storm Seraph

Lore: Six wings over a darkening sky. When a Storm Seraph descends, the air itself tilts — the moment before everything changes.

〰️

Mireheart Eel

Lore: It lives in black water and takes what drifts too close — warmth, light, life. Mireheart country is crossed quickly, and never alone.

Upgrades & Ranks

After every Trial, choose 1 of 3 boons: Faster Fire, +Damage, +Max Forge, Wider Bolts, or an Extra Life (once per run). End every run ranked S / A / B / C and earn Ember Shards of the First Spark.
