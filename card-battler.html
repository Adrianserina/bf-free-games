      How Ember Shards Work

      1 · Earn them by playing. Every Bloodline Forge game pays out Ember Shards of the First Spark — fragments of the Living Sigil. Wins and daily challenges pay the most; in this game, the first 300 a day pay in full and earnings halve past that.

      2 · One wallet, every game. Shards are shared across all three games on this device. Earn them at the war-table tonight and they are waiting in the other games tomorrow — the balance in the top bar is that one wallet.

      3 · Spend them in the Forge. Card backs, glows, titles, and creature skins here; 500 shards forge into 1 Sigil Fragment, the prestige currency that carries over between seasons. Shards themselves are earned, never bought. The Forge Store beside the shop also takes Forge Tokens — earned in-game today from campaign chapters, Faction Mastery, and quests, and spent on premium cards, frames, skins, and battle packs. Real-money token packs and the premium Season Pass arrive with the December launch, and real merch is on sale now at the Bloodline Forge Fourthwall shop, linked in the Forge Store.

      4 · Saved locally. Wallet, stats, streaks, and collection are saved in this browser — no account needed. Come back on the same device and your record is right where you left it.

        How the Battle Works

        Draw 10 cards, swap up to 2, then take turns playing one card at a time — or pass and lock in your score. Your hand carries over between rounds, so winning cheap matters. Between rounds both warbands draw 2 fresh cards. Win a round and your rival loses a gem; lose both gems and the match is gone. A drawn round costs both sides a gem.

        Abilities: Tight Bond multiplies bonded kin in the same row. Morale raises every other unit in its row. Muster calls its kin straight from your deck. Spies fight on the rival's row while you draw 2 cards. Medics raise the strongest fallen unit. Commander's Horn doubles a row — the Twelfth Trial sounds the same horn. Crown's Writ burns the strongest units on the whole field. Ashfall chokes every non-hero unit down to 1 strength. Heroes stand immune to Crown's Writ and Ashfall alike.

        Rewards: win a match for +75 Ember Shards of the First Spark (+25 for a draw) and +10 per round won. Rank points: +25 per win, −10 per loss, never below 0. Complete all three Daily Challenge conditions for a +100 shard bonus, multiplied by your daily streak (3 days ×1.5, 7 days ×2). The first 300 shards earned in this game each day pay in full; past that, match earnings pay half. Ember Shards of the First Spark are fragments of the Living Sigil — spent in the Forge, earned in the Trials.
