📅 Today's Daily Trial — Three Conditions

One Trial, three conditions, rotating daily. Win Daily Trial races to tick them off — check all three to complete the Trial and bank the bonus. Same seeded dice all day, for every player.

The copy includes a playable link carrying today's three conditions plus your real record from this device — no shared totals, nothing invented.

🏆 Forge Rank — Season I: The First Spark

Sigil Fragments: 0 — forged in the Shard Shop, 500 shards = 1 Fragment. Season reset, plainly: when a season ends, Forge Rank progress (lifetime shards, wins, Bestiary) starts over — but Sigil Fragments and every cosmetic you own carry over. Nothing you bought is ever lost.

📖 Bestiary — Creatures of the Road

Encounter each creature tile in a race to record it. Complete the Bestiary (100%) to unlock the Creature Token Skin in the Shard Shop.

🪶 Local Record — On This Device Only

👑 Champions — Codex & Unlocks

Six champions walk the road. Four are open from the start. Two are earned — win them on the road or forge them with shards or Forge Tokens. Locked champions show exactly how to unlock them.

🏆 Grand Tour — 12-Stage Campaign

A 12-race circuit through the chapter moments of the saga. Each stage names its rival and difficulty. Points: Win 10 · 2nd 6 · 3rd 3 — the stage winner takes 10, the runner-up 6, and the field's third-placed rival 3. After 12 stages the higher total takes the Tour. Progress saves on this device — pick any unlocked stage, chase your best result.

⚔️ Tournament — Single Elimination

Four enter, one takes the Forge Crown. Semi-final, then the Final — best-of-1, lose and you're out. The other semi is settled on the road while you race. Win the bracket for ✦ 300 + 300 XP and the title Forge Tournament Champion. Quit mid-tournament and the bracket waits for you here.

🪜 Rival Ladder — 8 Named Rivals

Eight rivals hold the ladder, each with a style of their own. Beat one to unlock the next. Cautious rivals waste gifts, aggressive swappers hunt the Seris tile, and the Lyra-hunter comes armed against Lyra herself. Ladder progress saves.

📜 Quests & XP — Forge Journey

Every race grants XP — win +100 XP · finish +25 XP — and feeds the quests below. All progress reads your real races on this device, shared through the bf-meta journey record the hub uses. Claim a finished quest to bank its shards, XP, and Forge Tokens (◈ 5 daily · ◈ 15 weekly).

How to Play — Rules of the Road

Each race is a Trial of the Forge Road. Start on Tile 1, the Ember Gate, race to Tile 40, the Living Sigil. Overshoot bounces — only an exact landing wins. Rolling a 6 earns 1 ember shard. Creature tiles fire only on a dice landing (no chaining).

  • Ember Cache +1 shard · Glasshound +3 · Lyra Vale roll again · Moonwraith −3 · Boneharrow −2 · Thornjaw lose a turn · Seris Thorne swap places
  • Lyra Vale — Second Chance: reroll a 1 once per game. Glasshound — Long Stride: +1 every move. Thornjaw — Trap-Proof Hide: immune to traps. Seris Thorne — Slip the Pull: ignores first harmful drag-back/swap.
  • Campaign: Trial I, Trial II, and Trial III — first to 2 Trial wins; shards carry between Trials; shop between Trials.
  • Daily Trial: same seeded dice pattern all day, but the Trial itself is three conditions — tick them off by winning Daily races (for example: win without a Shield Token blocking for you, win with the champion of the day, or land exactly on Tile 40 without bouncing). Check all three to complete it and bank a +100 bonus, multiplied by your daily streak: 3 days ×1.5, 7 days ×2. Its name turns with the day — Survive the Twelfth Trial, Silence the Bloodvine, Carry the Spark, The Glass Must Not Break.
