      How Ember Shards Work

      The economy, in plain words.

      • Earning: Forge Defense pays for Trials survived and bosses felled; The Forge Road pays for races finished and won; Card Battler pays for rounds and duels won. Daily challenges pay a 50-shard reward × your streak multiplier.

      • Daily soft cap: the first 300 shards earned each day pay at full rate. Past the cap, games pay half — so farming one game all day is never the best route. Rotating games and finishing dailies is.

        Earned today (tracked on this device)0 / 300

      • Spending: titles, card backs, road tokens, and forge glows in the Shop — plus one-use powers. Cosmetics are permanent and carry over every season.

      • Sigil Fragments are the prestige currency: 500 shards forge 1 Fragment in the Shop. Fragments never reset and mark a true Trial-Walker.
